Description:
This dataset contains customer-level information for an e-commerce platform, 
including demographic details (Age, Gender, City), purchase behavior (Total Spend, 
Items Purchased, Days Since Last Purchase), membership tiers (Membership Type), 
satisfaction metrics (Satisfaction Level, Average Rating), and promotional impact 
(Discount Applied).
